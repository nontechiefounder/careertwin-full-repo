# ModelWeave Labs Hiring Bar

The synthetic hiring bar prioritizes evaluation rigor, deployment evidence, and work-trial quality. Claims count only when attributable to a source. Implementation, ownership, architecture, and outcome responsibility are reviewed separately. Human approval is required before an external decision.

## Required behavior

Candidates may advance when the role contract is met with sufficient evidence. A narrow missing dimension should trigger a bounded proof request. Brand, prestige, identity, and protected information are excluded.
