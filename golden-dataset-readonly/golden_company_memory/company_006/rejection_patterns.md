# AegisPacket Rejection and Failure Patterns

Rejection history is descriptive, not truth. Stable job-relevant blockers include contradicted ownership and absent must-have evidence after a proportionate proof opportunity. Vague culture-fit, prestige, identity, and unsupported personality comments are excluded.

Advanced-but-failed cases frequently lacked depth in the role's highest-risk dimension. Hired-but-unsuccessful cases are retained with confounders rather than converted into universal disqualifiers. Offer declines are not performance failures.
